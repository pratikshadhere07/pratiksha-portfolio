Cypher Assistant

A smart Android voice assistant enabling completely hands-free calling, real-time command parsing, speech-driven contact dialing, and conversational AI for enhanced mobile accessibility.

The Challenge & Problem

Smartphone users frequently face significant friction when trying to execute critical mobile tasks while driving, working, cooking, or managing accessibility limitations. Standard touch interaction requires visual attention and physical dexterity that may not be feasible or safe.

Furthermore, default mobile assistants often suffer from high network latency, heavy memory overhead, or inability to perform direct background contact matching without constant user confirmations.

The Solution & Engineering

Cypher Assistant was engineered from the ground up as a native Android application combining the speed of local Android speech recognition services with the semantic reasoning of OpenAI APIs.

A lightweight background service actively detects wake commands with minimal battery consumption. When a voice instruction is captured, it is tokenized, intent-classified, and matched against an optimized SQLite/MySQL contact database, directly triggering the native Android dialer without touchscreen interaction.

Key Capabilities & Features

  • Hands-Free Automated Calling: Accurately resolves contact names and dials numbers instantly through voice commands.
  • OpenAI GPT Intelligence: Answers general queries, summarizes messages, and provides contextual conversational feedback.
  • Low-Power Background Wake Listener: Battery-optimized Android service capable of long idle standby.
  • Dynamic Audio Waveform Visualizer: Live microphone frequency animation providing intuitive visual responsiveness.
