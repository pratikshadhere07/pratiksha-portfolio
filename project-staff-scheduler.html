Staff Scheduling & Governance App

An enterprise Android mobile solution engineered to facilitate seamless timetable coordination, dynamic conflict detection, leave substitution pipelines, and official administrative broadcasts across academic faculty hierarchies.

The Institutional Friction

In large colleges and universities, timetable management is typically managed using static spreadsheets or manual notices. When a faculty member is sick or on duty, finding an available substitute in the same department requires urgent, stressful telephone chains.

Simultaneously, Heads of Department (HODs) and Principals lack real-time visibility into classroom occupancy, active lecture progress, and urgent communication delivery confirmations.

The Architectural Solution

The platform implements a role-based client-server architecture with three defined authority tiers: Faculty, HOD, and Principal.

A smart conflict-detection algorithm cross-references department timetables to automatically recommend available substitute teachers with a single tap. All approvals, schedule revisions, and official circulars are synchronized via a MySQL database and dispatched immediately through Firebase Cloud Messaging.

Core Features

  • Role-Specific Dashboards: Dedicated UI workflows for Faculty (Timetables & Leave), HOD (Substitution & Approval), and Principal (Broadcasts & Oversight).
  • Zero-Conflict Timetable Engine: Automatically flags and prevents overlapping classroom and instructor bookings.
  • 1-Tap Substitution Engine: Finds free faculty during required time slots and routes requests for instant mobile approval.
  • Push Delivery Circulars: Dispatches high-priority institutional memos with automated read-receipt verification.
